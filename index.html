❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked
Questions

How much does it cost to get a job abroad through JP Careers?
Our service fees vary by destination and program. Japan caregiver/nursing programs: ₹1.5–2.5 lakh including language training and visa. Canada/UK work visa: ₹80,000–1.5 lakh. Germany caregiver: ₹1.2–2 lakh. We offer flexible EMI plans. The initial consultation is completely free.
Do I need to know the local language to work abroad?
It depends on the country. For Japan and Germany, basic language skills (JLPT N4 or German A2/B1) are required but we provide full language training. For Canada, UK, and Australia, English proficiency (IELTS 6.5+) is typically sufficient. We train you for whatever is required.
What is the typical timeline from consultation to landing abroad?
The timeline varies: Canada Express Entry: 6–12 months. Japan (with language training): 12–18 months. Germany (study route): 6–12 months after language prep. UK Skilled Worker Visa: 4–8 months. We set realistic timelines and keep you updated throughout the process.
What qualifications do I need to work as a nurse in Japan?
To work as a nurse or caregiver in Japan, you need: an Indian nursing degree (BSc/GNM/ANM), Japanese language proficiency (JLPT N4 minimum, N3 preferred), clean medical records, and no criminal background. JP Careers provides all language training and handles the COE (Certificate of Eligibility) process.
Is Germany really free for Indian students?
Yes! All 17 German states have abolished tuition fees at public universities, including for international students. You only pay a semester administrative fee (€150–350). You need a blocked account showing €11,208 for the student visa, plus living expenses of €700–1,000/month. Many students work part-time (20 hrs/week allowed) to cover costs.
What documents are required for a Canada work permit?
For Canada Express Entry: valid passport, educational credential assessment (ECA), IELTS score (CLB 7+), proof of work experience, police clearance certificate, medical exam results, and proof of funds. We assist with every document and guide you through the entire Express Entry profile creation.
Can JP Careers guarantee a job placement?
While no ethical agency can guarantee employment (as it depends on your performance in interviews), we have a 92% placement rate for candidates who complete our full program. We provide direct employer connections, multiple interview opportunities, and continue supporting you until you land a confirmed position.
Do you provide post-arrival support in the destination country?
Yes! Our support doesn't end when you board the flight. We provide: pre-departure briefing, airport pickup coordination (Japan/Germany), local bank account setup guidance, sim card & housing assistance tips, and a WhatsApp community of JP Careers alumni in your destination city. You're never alone.
