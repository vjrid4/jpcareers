📖 What It Is

What Is Career
Counselling?

Career counselling is a professional guidance process where we help you understand your strengths, interests, and market opportunities — and then align them into a clear, actionable plan for your overseas career.

Unlike generic advice on YouTube or friends who "went abroad," our counsellors use validated assessments and deep market knowledge to give you highly personalized guidance. We've counselled 5,000+ individuals over 15 years.

Whether you're a fresh graduate choosing between studying in Germany vs Canada, or a 10-year experienced nurse wondering about Japan vs UK — we give you clarity, not confusion.

📋 Our Process

How Career Counselling
Works at JP Careers

1

Free Intro Call (30 min)

Share your background, goals, and concerns. We listen before we advise. No sales pitch — just honest conversation.

2

Assessment & Analysis (45 min)

Complete our online psychometric and career interest assessments. We analyze your academic record, experience, and financial situation.

3

Career Roadmap Presentation

We present 2–3 tailored options with country, job role, timeline, estimated cost, and projected earnings. You ask questions, we answer honestly.

4

Decision & Action Plan

Once you choose your path, we create a month-by-month action plan with milestones — language training, applications, visa, departure.
