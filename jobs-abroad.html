General
Requirements

What you typically need to work abroad. Specific requirements depend on your target country and job role.

Documents Required

  • ✓ Valid passport (2+ years validity)
  • ✓ Educational certificates & transcripts
  • ✓ Work experience certificates
  • ✓ IELTS / language test results
  • ✓ Police clearance certificate
  • ✓ Medical fitness certificate
  • ✓ Updated CV / resume

Language Requirements

🇯🇵 Japan

JLPT N4 minimum. N3 preferred for better opportunities.

🇩🇪 Germany

German B1 for caregiving. B2 for engineering/IT roles.

🇨🇦 Canada / 🇬🇧 UK / 🇦🇺 Australia

IELTS 6.5+ (CLB 7). Some roles require 7.0+.

🇳🇿 New Zealand

IELTS 6.5+ overall (no band below 6.0). Nursing/healthcare roles may require 7.0+.

🇮🇪 Ireland

English proficiency — IELTS 6.0+ for general work. Nursing roles require IELTS 7.0+ (all bands 6.5+).

🇳🇱 Netherlands / 🇧🇪 Belgium

English B2 for tech/logistics roles. Dutch A2–B1 is an advantage for client-facing jobs.

🇫🇷 France / 🇪🇸 Spain / 🇵🇹 Portugal

French/Spanish/Portuguese B1–B2 required. DELF / DELE certifications preferred by employers.

🇵🇱 Poland / 🇨🇿 Czech Republic / 🇷🇴 Romania

English B1+ accepted for most manufacturing, IT & logistics roles. Local language training available.

🇸🇪 Sweden / 🇩🇰 Denmark / 🇳🇴 Norway / 🇫🇮 Finland

English proficiency (IELTS 6.0+) sufficient for most roles. Scandinavian language is a strong bonus.
