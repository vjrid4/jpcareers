📖 Our Story

From Andhra Pradesh
to the World

JP Careers was founded in 2022 by B Priyanka with a clear vision: to give ambitious Indians from Andhra Pradesh and beyond the right guidance to build careers without borders.

What started as a focused consulting practice in Andhra Pradesh is rapidly growing into a trusted overseas career platform, with expertise across Japan, Germany, Canada, UK, Australia, New Zealand, Ireland, and Europe.

Today, we're proud to have placed 2,400+ candidates across 50+ countries, maintained a 98% visa success rate, and built a community of thriving overseas professionals who call us family.

✅ Why Choose Us

What Makes JP Careers
Different

End-to-End Support

From your first consultation to your first day at work abroad — we're with you. No hand-offs, no middlemen. Your dedicated counsellor handles everything.

Verified Employers Only

We work only with government-registered, OISD-approved, and directly verified employers. Zero scam risk — we've turned down 200+ unverified employers to protect our candidates.

Transparent Pricing

No hidden fees, ever. All costs are disclosed upfront before you sign anything. We offer EMI options and some programs have success-based fee structures.

Alumni Network

Access our active community of 2,400+ alumni across Japan, Germany, Canada, UK, Australia, and the USA for mentorship, housing tips, and genuine firsthand advice.

In-House Language Training

Unlike most agencies, our language training is done in-house by certified Japanese, German, and English trainers — ensuring quality consistency and faster learning outcomes.

Fast Processing

Our streamlined process and existing relationships with embassies and employers mean your application moves faster. Japan COE in 60 days. Canada ITA within 3 months for eligible profiles.
