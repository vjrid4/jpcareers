Germany — Study for Free

Zero Tuition at Public Universities

Germany is the world's best-kept education secret. All 17 German states have abolished tuition fees for international students, including Indians. You only pay a semester fee of €150–350. Top universities like TU Munich, LMU Munich, Heidelberg, and KIT are completely free.

Popular programs: Engineering (mechanical, electrical, automotive), Computer Science, MBA (in English), Environmental Science, Medicine. Most programs are available in English, though learning German (B2 level) opens far more options and better job prospects post-graduation.

Free Tuition IELTS 6.5+ 18 months Work Rights Post-Study Blocked Account Required

Key Facts

  • ✓Tuition: €0 (public universities)
  • ✓Living costs: €700–1,000/month
  • ✓Blocked account: €11,208
  • ✓Work rights: 120 days/year
  • ✓Post-study: 18 months job seeker
  • ✓PR: Possible after 5 years

Canada — PGWP & PR Pathway

Strong PR Pathway via Express Entry

Canada is one of the most immigrant-friendly countries for Indian students. After graduating from a designated learning institution (DLI), you get a Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) for up to 3 years — during which you can gain Canadian work experience and apply for PR through Express Entry.

Popular universities: University of Toronto, UBC, McGill, University of Waterloo, York University. Affordable community colleges in Ontario and BC also offer strong programs with PGWP eligibility. Popular programs: IT, Data Science, Business, Engineering, Nursing.

IELTS 6.5+ PGWP up to 3 Years PR via Express Entry Part-time Work Allowed

Key Facts

  • ✓Tuition: CAD 15,000–35,000/yr
  • ✓Living costs: CAD 1,200–1,800/mo
  • ✓Work rights: 20 hrs/week during
  • ✓Post-study: PGWP 1–3 years
  • ✓PR: Possible in 3–4 years
  • ✓Scholarships available

Australia — 485 Graduate Visa

World's 3rd Most Popular Study Destination

Australia offers excellent post-study work rights and a world-class lifestyle. After completing a degree at an Australian university, you're eligible for the Temporary Graduate Visa (subclass 485) which allows you to work for 2–5 years depending on your field and state of study.

Top universities: University of Melbourne, Australian National University, University of Sydney, Monash University. Popular programs: Nursing (high demand), MBA, Engineering, IT, Accounting. Many Indian students transition to skilled migration after their post-study work visa.

IELTS 6.5+ 485 Visa 2–5 Years Unlimited Work Hours PR via Skilled Migration

Key Facts

  • ✓Tuition: AUD 20,000–45,000/yr
  • ✓Living costs: AUD 1,500–2,200/mo
  • ✓Work rights: 48 hrs/fortnight
  • ✓Post-study: 485 visa 2–5 yrs
  • ✓Scholarships: Multiple options
  • ✓Safe, welcoming environment

United Kingdom — 2-Year PSW Visa

World-Renowned Degrees, 2 Years Post-Study Work

The UK offers globally prestigious degrees (Oxford, Cambridge, Imperial, UCL) in a relatively short duration — most UK degrees are 3 years for undergrad and 1 year for Masters. Post-study, the Graduate Route Visa gives 2 years (3 for PhD) to work and gain experience.

Popular programs: Finance, Law, Business Management, Engineering, Computer Science, Nursing. The NHS connection makes nursing studies in the UK particularly attractive — many students get sponsored for their nursing degree and secure NHS jobs directly.

IELTS 6.0–7.0 Graduate Route 2 Years NHS Sponsorship World Top Universities

Key Facts

  • ✓Tuition: £12,000–25,000/yr
  • ✓Living costs: £1,000–1,500/mo
  • ✓Work rights: 20 hrs/week during
  • ✓Post-study: Graduate Route 2 yrs
  • ✓Chevening Scholarships available
  • ✓1-year Masters programs

USA — F-1 Student Visa & OPT

Top Research Universities & STEM OPT Extension

The USA hosts the world's top research universities and is the most popular destination for Indian STEM students. The F-1 student visa allows 1 year of post-study work (OPT), which extends to 3 years for STEM graduates — giving you time to secure H-1B sponsorship.

Universities: MIT, Stanford, Carnegie Mellon, Purdue, Texas A&M. Scholarships and teaching assistantships are widely available for Masters programs. Popular programs: Computer Science, Data Science, Electrical Engineering, MBA, Biotechnology.

IELTS 6.5+ or TOEFL 80+ STEM OPT 3 Years GRE/GMAT Required TA/RA Scholarships

Key Facts

  • ✓Tuition: USD 20,000–55,000/yr
  • ✓Living costs: USD 1,200–2,000/mo
  • ✓On-campus work: 20 hrs/week
  • ✓OPT: 1 year (3 yr STEM)
  • ✓H-1B sponsorship pathway
  • ✓World-class research facilities

Study Abroad
FAQ

What IELTS score do I need to study abroad?
Requirements vary by country and university. Generally: Germany 6.0–6.5, Canada 6.5, Australia 6.5, UK 6.0–7.0, USA 6.5–7.0 (TOEFL 80–100). We help you target scores based on your desired universities.
Can I work while studying abroad?
Yes! Germany allows 120 days/year (or 240 half days). Canada allows 20 hours/week. Australia allows 48 hours per fortnight. UK allows 20 hours/week. USA allows 20 hours/week (on-campus). This work income significantly reduces your overall expenses.
What is the minimum GPA needed for study abroad?
For Germany, a 60–65% aggregate is generally sufficient for most programs. For Canada and Australia, 65–70% is standard. For top UK universities, 70%+ is preferred. We always find the right university fit regardless of your percentage — there are excellent options at all academic levels.
How long does the university admission process take?
From starting your application to receiving an offer letter typically takes 2–6 months depending on the country and university. German public universities: 4–8 weeks decision time. Canadian and Australian universities: 4–12 weeks. We submit to multiple universities simultaneously to ensure you get offers.
Are there scholarships available for Indian students?
Absolutely! Many universities offer merit scholarships worth 20–100% of tuition. Key scholarships: DAAD (Germany), Chevening (UK), Commonwealth (Australia), Vanier (Canada). Government of India also offers National Overseas Scholarship. We actively help identify and apply for relevant scholarships.
